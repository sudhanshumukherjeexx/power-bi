# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The summary below is for convenience; the full text is the reference.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of:

- age, body size or visible or invisible disability;
- ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression;
- level of experience, education or socio-economic status;
- nationality, personal appearance, race, caste or colour;
- religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Many people here are learning. Questions that seem basic are welcome, and answers should be kind.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback on content and code, not on people.
- Credit others' work.
- Focus on what is best for learners.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer through the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile (https://github.com/sudhanshumukherjeexx). Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may respond with any of the following, in proportion to the behaviour:

- correction;
- warning;
- temporary ban;
- permanent ban.
