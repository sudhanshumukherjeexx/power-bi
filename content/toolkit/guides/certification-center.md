## How to use this

Microsoft's study guide is the authority on what the exam measures; this page is the navigator from each objective to practice. For each objective: **learn** it in the linked lessons, **drill** it with the assignments and flashcards, then **use** it in a scenario. Your measured confidence per area is on the [certification map](learn.html#certs).

> **Note:** Certification and job skill overlap but aren't the same. Exams test breadth of features; jobs test judgement under messy conditions. Do both: the exam opens doors, the scenarios get you through them.

## Study plan

1. Read the official study guide and mark each objective: confident, unsure, never seen.
2. For "never seen", do the linked lessons and their assignments first.
3. Daily flashcards (10–15 minutes) on the exam's categories; let spaced repetition pick the order.
4. One scenario a week that uses the exam's skills, so the knowledge sticks.
5. Take Microsoft's free practice assessment a week before the exam; revisit the weakest areas.
