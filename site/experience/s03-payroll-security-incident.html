Sam built the Sales & Payroll model last quarter so regional managers could see sales and their own team's pay in one place. It was published as an app with an audience per region.

Why the business cares Salary data is personal data. A manager seeing other teams' pay is a privacy breach that Compliance may have to log, and it damages trust between managers and HR. Until it is contained, every day adds people who might have seen something they shouldn't.

What you are expected to do

  • Contain the exposure within the first hour, without breaking the app for everyone else. Record what you did and when.
  • Reproduce the problem with View as role (or by querying as the user) and capture the evidence.
  • Find every reason the security didn't hold. There may be more than one.
  • Fix the model so security is enforced by the engine, not by the report.
  • Build an RLS/OLS test matrix covering each role and a representative user, and run it.
  • Classify the severity, write the incident notes Ava asked for, and list preventive actions.

Deliverables

  • Containment action and timeline (what, who, when)
  • Root cause analysis: each weakness, with evidence
  • The corrected roles (TMDL or screenshots of Manage roles)
  • RLS/OLS test matrix: user, role, expected regions, forbidden regions, payroll visible?, expected total, actual, pass/fail
  • Notes for Compliance: exposure window, who could see what, how we know it's fixed

Evidence

  • roles.tmdl (from the published model) The model's security roles as currently deployed.
  • relationships.csv All relationships in the model, including cross-filter direction and whether they are active.
  • security_user_region.csv SecurityUserRegion table: which user may see which region.
  • employees.csv
  • payroll_q1_2026.csv FactPayroll: monthly gross pay and bonus per employee.
  • report_page_filters.csv Filters configured in the report (exported from the PBIR files).
  • activity_log.csv Activity events for the model and app since Monday.
  • App configuration
