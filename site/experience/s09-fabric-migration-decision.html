The certified model from Sprint 05 now serves 140 people. Refresh takes 74 minutes and is creeping up; the operations dashboard still uses DirectQuery against the warehouse management system, and Sprint 08 showed how fragile the on-premises chain is.

Why the business cares This decision fixes the platform, cost and skills the BI team will live with for three to five years. Getting it wrong means either an expensive migration that changes nothing for users, or a cheap one that hits a wall in a year.

ADR-009: target architecture

Our P1 capacity won't be renewed in December: Microsoft is retiring Premium per-capacity SKUs, and the equivalent is an F64 Fabric capacity. That forces a decision, so let's make a good one.

Please write ADR-009 for the architecture board on the 23rd: options, constraints, trade-offs, your recommendation, consequences, risks and what would make us revisit it. Everything you need is in the folder.

I don't want the most modern option. I want the one this team can run.

  • Record your decision (Your work → Decision) and write ADR-009.
  • Explain the recommendation to the CFO in five sentences and to Kenji in technical terms.
