You are now leading BI architecture at Northwind. The shared revenue model (Sprint 05), the deployment checks (Sprint 07), the incident process (Sprint 08) and ADR-009 (Sprint 09) all exist, but only for the parts of the estate the BI team touches.

Why the business cares Every unmanaged copy of a model is a possible wrong number, a possible data leak and a refresh that costs capacity. The offsite will decide the data programme's budget; a vague review gets a vague budget.

Ownership

From a compliance point of view my main concern is items with personal or financial data that have no named owner. Please flag those.

What you are expected to do

  • Assess the current state with numbers: duplication, ownership, endorsement, usage, refresh health, revenue and customer definitions.
  • Design the target: workspace strategy, model consolidation, endorsement and ownership, security, and how it fits ADR-009.
  • Define a light governance model that people will actually follow.
  • Plan a 12-month roadmap with phases, owners and risks. Nothing is deleted without checking dependencies and telling owners.
  • Write the one-page executive recommendation for the offsite.

Deliverables

  • Current-state assessment with numbers
  • Target architecture and workspace strategy
  • Governance model: roles, endorsement criteria, ownership rules
  • Consolidation and retirement plan
  • 12-month roadmap with risks
  • One-page executive recommendation

Evidence

  • workspaces.csv
  • semantic_models.csv Owner, endorsement, revenue measure name, customer table name, RLS and last refresh status.
  • reports.csv Every report with its model and views in the last 90 days.
  • What already exists from earlier work
